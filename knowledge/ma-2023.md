## shows
Meta-analysis of 10 studies of habit formation interventions for physical activity: they significantly increased habit strength (SMD = 0.31, 95% CI 0.14-0.48). Follow-up of 12 weeks or less showed a larger effect than longer follow-up. In meta-regression, problem solving was associated with greater effectiveness (beta = 0.36, 95% CI 0.17-0.55).
## limits
Only 10 studies and only physical activity; the outcome is habit strength, not long-term behaviour. The association of social reward with lower effectiveness (beta = -0.40) has a confidence interval that reaches zero (-0.74 to 0.06), so it is weak evidence.
