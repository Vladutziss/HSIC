## shows
Across seven studies, highlighting intact streaks in a behaviour log increased later engagement compared with highlighting broken streaks, depending on how past behaviour was represented rather than on what was actually done. People treat maintaining a logged streak as a goal in itself. The effect was stronger when people attributed the break to their own actions and weaker when they could repair a broken streak.
## limits
Experiments on how streaks are displayed; the abstract reports no long-term field trial of a habit app and no teenage sample. The abstract does not state limitations.
