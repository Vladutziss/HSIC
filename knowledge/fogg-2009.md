## shows
Behaviour happens when three elements converge at the same moment: motivation, ability and a trigger. Motivation and ability can offset each other, so high motivation can compensate for low ability, and making a behaviour easier raises the chance it happens.
## limits
A conceptual design model, not an experimental result; later versions renamed the trigger "prompt" (B=MAP).
