## shows
Meta-analysis of 642 tests of implementation intentions (if-then plans). They were effective for cognitive, affective and behavioural outcomes (.27 <= d <= .66). Effects were larger for plans in the if-then format, when people were highly motivated, and when plans were rehearsed.
## limits
d is a standardised effect size, not a percentage of people who succeed. The pooled studies cover many populations and behaviours; the abstract does not single out teenagers or app users. It is not a guarantee for any one person.
