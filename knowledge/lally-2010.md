## shows
Participants chose a daily eating, drinking or activity behaviour and performed it in the same context for 12 weeks, reporting automaticity daily. For the 39 of 62 participants whose data fitted the model well, the time to reach 95% of the asymptote of automaticity ranged from 18 to 254 days. Missing one opportunity to perform the behaviour did not materially affect the habit formation process.
## limits
Depends on self-reported automaticity, and a good model fit was found for 39 of the 62 modelled participants. The "21 days" figure has no support here; the range shows large individual differences.
