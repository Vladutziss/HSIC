## shows
If-then plans link an anticipated situation to a planned response, so that the response is triggered more automatically when the situation occurs. The paper summarises a research programme showing that such plans improve goal attainment.
## limits
A review by the researcher who proposed the idea; it does not give one pooled effect size. For effect sizes use sheeran-2025.
