## shows
Habits form gradually from links between responses and the contexts in which they were repeatedly performed; once formed, a habit can be triggered by the context alone, without a mediating goal. Goals can help build habits through repetition and by exposing people to habit-triggering cues. The paper discusses changing habits by self-regulating habit cues.
## limits
A theoretical integration, not a trial of an intervention.
