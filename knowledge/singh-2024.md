## shows
Systematic review and meta-analysis of 20 studies with 2601 participants. Four studies reported time to habit formation: medians of 59-66 days and means of 106-154 days, with individual variability from 4 to 335 days. Habit scores rose from pre to post intervention (SMD 0.69, 95% CI 0.49-0.88). Frequency, timing, type of habit and individual choice influenced habit strength; morning practices and self-selected habits generally showed greater strength.
## limits
11 of the 20 studies had a high risk of bias, the time-to-habit figures rest on only four studies, and flossing and diet had the most evidence. "About two months" is an order of magnitude, not a deadline.
