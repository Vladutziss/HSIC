## shows
In a randomised study, participants received self-compassion psychoeducation or a control, then responded to a goal lapse they recalled. The education seemed to change beliefs about self-compassion, but there was no evidence that it increased self-compassionate responding to the lapse.
## limits
A null result on the response outcome; the sample size is not in the abstract. It does not show that self-compassion is useless, only that telling people about it was not enough; the authors say other strategies are needed.
