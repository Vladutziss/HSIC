## shows
Three archival field studies found that Google searches for "diet", gym visits at a university gym and goal commitments on stickK.com rose after temporal landmarks such as the start of a new week, month, year or semester, and after birthdays and holidays. The authors propose that fresh starts create a sense of separation from past selves and past failures.
## limits
Archival, observational data from adults and university students; it shows an association with landmarks, not that a plan made on a landmark works better for a given person. No replication from 2022 onward was found.
