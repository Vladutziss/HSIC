## shows
Machine-learning analysis of objective data: gym attendance (over 12 million observations) and hospital handwashing (over 40 million). Contrary to the belief in a "magic number" of days, forming the gym habit typically took months, while the handwashing habit took weeks. Gymgoers who were more predictable were less responsive to an intervention designed to increase attendance.
## limits
Two specific behaviours in adults (gym members, hospital workers); the abstract does not generalise to other habits or to teenagers. Time to habit is inferred from how predictable behaviour becomes, not from self-report.
