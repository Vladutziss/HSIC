Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. What this is

Cookies are small files saved in your browser. We also use similar technologies (mainly localStorage) and treat them the same way. This policy says exactly what we store on your device, why, and how to change your choice. Personal data as such is described in the Privacy Policy. The operator is the one named there.

2. What we ask for and what we do not

Strictly necessary items are stored without your consent because the service you ask for does not work without them (Article 4(5) of Romanian Law 506/2004, implementing Directive 2002/58/EC). Analytics items are stored only if you accept them. Until you choose, nothing is stored and nothing is sent to the analytics tool. We use no advertising or marketing cookies and we do not sell data.

5. Error monitoring

We use Sentry to find out when the app breaks (the error message, the page, the browser, load times). It does not store cookies on your device, so it does not need consent as a cookie. The processing is described in the Privacy Policy.

6. How to change your choice

  • In the app: Settings → Cookies. You can accept or decline at any time, with immediate effect, and declining is as easy as accepting.
  • In your browser: you can delete or block cookies in the browser settings. Blocking the strictly necessary ones may prevent signing in.

7. Changes and contact

If we change what we store, we update this page and, where the law requires it, ask for your choice again. Questions: molted.app@gmail.com. Complaints can be filed with ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro.
